Our Programs

Discover a world of imagination and self-expression. We offer comprehensive theater programs tailored to specific age groups, ensuring the perfect balance of challenge and support.

Ages 4-6

Little Stars

A joyful introduction to theater using games, storytelling, and imaginative play to build confidence and coordination.

  • • Storybook roleplay
  • • Expressive movement
  • • Voice projection basics
View Schedule
Ages 7-10

Young Performers

Diving into actual scripts, students learn the fundamentals of stagecraft, character work, and working as an ensemble.

  • • Script reading & analysis
  • • Basic improvisation
  • • Stage blocking
View Schedule
Ages 11-14

Junior Actors

A deep dive into emotional depth, comedic timing, and dramatic scene work to prepare for more complex roles.

  • • Scene study
  • • Advanced character development
  • • Audition preparation
View Schedule
Ages 15-18

Teen Stage

A rigorous program for serious performers focusing on mastering classical and contemporary texts, monologues, and full productions.

  • • Classical text (Shakespeare)
  • • Method acting techniques
  • • Directing basics
View Schedule

The Complete Curriculum

Our holistic approach ensures that students develop all necessary skills to become versatile, confident performers.

Voice & Diction

Breath control, projection, and articulation.

Stage Movement

Physicality, spatial awareness, and choreography.

Improvisation

Thinking on your feet and spontaneous creativity.

Character Work

Developing backstory, emotion, and objectives.

Script Analysis

Breaking down scenes and understanding subtext.

Stage Show

Bringing it all together for a live audience.
